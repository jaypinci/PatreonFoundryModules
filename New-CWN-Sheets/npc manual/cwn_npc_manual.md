# CHROME & SHADOWS
## A Cities Without Number NPC Compendium

*100 Street-Ready Threats, Contacts, and Characters for the Neon Future*

---

> **How to Read These Entries**
>
> **HD** — Hit Dice (roll d8 per HD for HP, or use average)  
> **AC** — Armor Class  
> **Atk** — Attack bonus to hit  
> **Dmg** — Damage per hit  
> **Move** — Movement speed (standard is 30 ft)  
> **ML** — Morale (2d6; fail = retreat/surrender)  
> **Save** — Base saving throw  
> **Skills** — Key skill bonuses  
> **Foci** — Special abilities and training  
> **Gear** — Standard loadout  
> **Tactics** — How they fight  

*NPC stats use the CWN/SWN condensed format. HD values assume combat-capable adults. Adjust HP and skills to taste.*
